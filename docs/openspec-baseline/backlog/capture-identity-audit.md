## Register reassessment and exclusion treatment

The ordered source traces above recheck the roots of U01/U02/U05/U08–U28/U53–U88, RS-U01–09 and AI-U01–05. The full original wording and occurrences remain in discrepancy-register.md. Shared U10 (mock qualification), U11 (runtime/device) and U12 (separate stores) are evidence limitations, not automatically bugs. U22 is historical authorization/acceptance provenance, not a present application malfunction. U80's absent “Needs review” route is an intentional naming distinction: the actual Reported/Ready to review filters exist, so no invented missing-screen backlog. U09 placeholders are excluded from positive feature claims; their connectedness and intended retirement need owner review rather than assumed synthesis functionality.

No new supported extraction omission was found in these five owners after checking the connected actions above. This is bounded source coverage, not a guarantee that every error timing, provider, browser, contract branch or deployment has been qualified. Existing defects are not drafted as desired policy.
