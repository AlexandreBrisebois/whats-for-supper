## Failure, interruption and retry

A→B and B→C are separate atomic DB transitions. Crash/cancellation before a commit
leaves the prior state; crash/lost reply during commit is unknown until a fresh process
reads it. Never infer rollback from an exception or rerun conversion blindly.
Classify A/B/C from complete facts and independently retained bytes; partial, mixed,
foreign or out-of-delta state rejects. Exact retry at A may create the same plan;
at B may commit the same prepared candidate once; at C returns the validated retained
outcome without repeated conversion, ID allocation, UpdatedAt/search writes or
historical edits. A pending producer witness can resume only the same candidate
through the existing validation owner. Completed task evidence is never overwritten.
A changed request/attempt/selection/date/candidate/database/root/schema/witness under
an old operation is a divergent retry and rejects. A fresh candidate requires fresh
bindings and a fresh disposable A, not relabeling B/C evidence.

Session loss, revoked/escaped context, wrong transaction/graph/lease, deadlines and
concurrent invocation invalidate the capability. Maintain the durable hold even after
failure. Mutation after commit discovered by D yields a blocked/divergent result,
not retroactive rollback or successful acceptance. No compensating repair, destructive
cleanup, source recapture or new baseline is implicit. Missing authority blocks only
dependent claims; preserve every failed attempt and exact first observed state.

Historical writer/build/serializer and original wire identity remain unproven.
No source inspection, synthetic fixture, rollback-only TEMP validation or predecessor
success substitutes for actual future producer/commit/fresh-process read-back evidence.
