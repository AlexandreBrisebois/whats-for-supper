# Bilingual reference evaluation — 2026-11-07

No dependency or dataset is selected/installed. These are primary-source research findings, not a claim that an entire dictionary is safe for direct grocery equivalence. Candidate counts, complete downloaded schema and corpus performance remain unmeasured.

Supplementary [research dated 2026-11-08](research-2026-11-08.md) examines reuse of existing category records and recipe terms, source quality, regional ambiguity and reuse limits. It records local snapshot evidence and nonnormative suggestions; it does not select a dataset, adopt vocabulary or add product requirements.

| Source | Verified primary evidence | Fit and adoption constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Health Canada CNF 2026 | [Official description](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/food-nutrition/healthy-eating/nutrient-data/canadian-nutrient-file-about-us.html) describes a bilingual food composition reference with downloadable relational files; [dataset record](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/1b6139bd-ed7e-4043-bc28-ff00e10f3109) lists EN/FR Food Name CSV and Open Government Licence – Canada | Best initial EN/FR pilot. Composition descriptions are not automatically shopper synonyms; cooked/raw, composite foods, preparations and purchase forms need review. Dataset listing verifies stated licence; direct licence page returned 403 in this research session, so retain/review the exact licence text and attribution conditions before adoption. |
| ANSES Ciqual | [Official site](https://ciqual.anses.fr/) returned 503 during this session | Potential FR terminology cross-check; language fields, version, usable export and exact reuse terms not verified here. Do not copy third-party licence claims or adopt until official download/terms are obtained. |
| EFSA FoodEx2 | [Official classification explanation](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/data/data-standardisation) distinguishes hierarchical food concepts and facets; [collection guide](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/resources/data-collection) links catalogue tools | Useful distinction/qualifier audit; a broad/narrow hierarchy is not an exact alias dictionary. EN/FR coverage and catalogue-specific redistribution terms remain unverified; not an automatic shopping crosswalk. |
| FooDB | [Official site](https://www.foodb.ca/) and [downloads](https://foodb.ca/downloads) state noncommercial licensing and commercial permission requirements | Composition/chemical focus, unverified bilingual coverage and reuse restrictions make it unsuitable as an assumed permissive production seed. No use without resolved rights and measured fit. |

Recommended evaluation: pin CNF Food Name CSV and full source package/version/hash, retain raw external codes as provenance, sample purchase distinctions and ambiguous kitchen terms in both languages, then curate only exact mappings into app IDs. Record mapped/unmapped/conflicting counts and false merges separately; a high coverage percentage cannot excuse fresh/canned or allergen/product collapse. Include poivron/piment, coriander leaf/seed, cream regional terms, flours, broth/concentrate and package forms. No acceptance threshold is asserted without measurement. No runtime network lookup is required once approved facts are saved.

Governance proposal: retain source/version/licence and attribution with each imported term; two-language meaning review before publishing labels/aliases; same-key collisions remain unresolved. Releases carry reviewed affected mappings/qualifiers/manual-category conflicts and reversal mapping. Saved human corrections remain protected, private and household-scoped. Upstream refresh never silently changes saved equivalence or checked-row identity. Add source attribution output appropriate to adopted terms and preserve provenance in current-format recovery.

Cooklang is inspiration rather than a dependency: [conventions](https://cooklang.org/docs/conventions/) distinguish servings from yield and describe scaling; [syntax](https://cooklang.org/docs/spec/) separates ingredients, cookware and timers. WFS keeps unknown serving basis and ordinary text editing and explicitly separates step use from grocery demand. No parser/library or Cooklang demand-counting semantics are adopted.
