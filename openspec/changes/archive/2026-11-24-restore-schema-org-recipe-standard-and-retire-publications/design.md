# Design

## Context and Existing Ownership

- **Disk recipe storage (`IRecipeStore`, `LocalRecipeStore`, `RecipeRepository`)**: Each recipe owns `{RecipesRoot}/{id}/` containing `recipe.info` (household metadata: `id`, `addedBy`, `rating`, `notes`, `imageCount`, `finishedDishImageIndex`, rotation flags), `recipe.json` (recipe content), optional `hero.webp`, optional `original/*`, and optional `search.index.json`.
- **`dockerhub/v0.1.40` baseline & `./data/recipes/` layout**:
  - In `v0.1.40` (`99f553c4`), `recipes` stored `raw_metadata` (`https://schema.org/Recipe` JSON) and `ingredients` (JSON string array), without `servings`, `canonical_content`, or `food_mapping_evidence`. Nightly `db-backup` (`SerializeRecipeBackup`) wrote `{id}/recipe.json` with `"backupContentVersion": 1` and string-escaped `rawMetadata` — matching all 198 files in `./data/recipes/*/recipe.json` (each containing `@context`, `@type`, `name`, `recipeYield`, `totalTime`, `recipeIngredient`, `recipeInstructions` `HowToSection`s, `supply`, `nutrition`, `suggestedPairing`).
  - Container startup (`api/database/migrate.sh` → `compatibility.sql` → `sqldef` `schema.sql`, then `Program.cs` lines 434–438) runs `RecoverySnapshotService.ResumeIfInterruptedAsync` → `FoodVocabularySeeder.EnsureSeededAsync` → `RecipeStartupMigrator.MigrateAsync` **before** HTTP traffic and `WorkflowWorker` begin.
- **Regressions & coupling introduced by the Nov 8 canonical/publication checkpoint (`3f36eb34`)**:
  1. **Strict suffix & food-alignment gates**: `CanonicalRecipeContentStore.ConvertLines` (`api/src/RecipeApi/Services/CanonicalRecipeContentStore.cs:281-330`) throws `InvalidDataException("Trusted legacy facts cannot be represented losslessly; conversion deferred.")` whenever `recipeIngredient[i]` differs from `supply` in casing, `of`/`de`/`d'` prepositions, EN/FR unit aliases, or preparation notes; and `FoodMappingQualification.SourceAligned` (`api/src/RecipeApi/Services/FoodMappingQualification.cs:173-189`) skips bilingual `FoodId` matching unless `recipeIngredient[i] == Render(line)` and `supply[i].name == line.Shopping.Name`. In addition, `CategorizeRecipeProcessor` (`api/src/RecipeApi/Services/Processors/CategorizeRecipeProcessor.cs:57-59`) omits `"goto-synthesis"`.
  2. **Pervasive `RecipeFilePublicationStore` & launch-authority coupling**: Beyond `RecipeFilePublicationStore*.cs` (15 partials), `RecipePublicationCapture` and `RecipeFilePublication` are required by `WorkflowWorker.HasExecutionAuthority`, `WorkflowOrchestrator.Launches.cs` (`AcceptOriginalLaunchAsync`, `InitializeOriginalLaunch`), `WorkflowProcessorHistory.cs` (`Begin`, `ObserveAsync`), `SyncRecipeProcessor.cs` (throws when `publications.Count == 0`), `RecipeReadyProcessor.cs`, `RecipeHeroAgent.cs`, `WebAcquisitionAgent.cs`, `PdfConversionProcessor.cs`, `PdfCaptureService.cs`, `RecipeAgent.cs` (`ExtractRecipe`, `GenerateDescription`, `SynthesizeRecipe`), `RecipeService.cs` (`CreateRecipe`, `DescribeRecipe`, `CaptureUrl`, `ImportRecipeShareBundle`, `AddOriginalImageAsync`), `RecipeImportService.cs`, `RecipeImportReportService.cs`, and `RecipeReportRepairIntent.cs`.
  3. **`ManagementService` & `RecoverySnapshotService` wrapper traps**:
     - `RecipeExportContent.Current.cs` writes `"format": "wfs-recipe", "version": 2` while also housing in-memory `ValidateCurrentContent` (`86-156`).
     - `ManagementService.CanonicalBackup.cs` writes `data/canonical-backup.json` and requires `wfs-recipe` v2 during `RestoreAsync` (`PreflightCanonicalSeedAsync`).
     - `ManagementService.RestoreAsync` (`1130-1139`) blocks merge updates whenever `existing.CanonicalContent is not null`, leaves `CanonicalContent = null` when restoring `SchemaOrgRecipe` / `v0.1.40` files, skips structured recipes in `BackfillFinishedDishIndicesAsync` (`1562`), and skips `recipe.info` in `DisasterRecoveryAsync` (`1639-1649`) whenever `recipe.json` exists.
     - `RecoverySnapshotService.Restore.cs:322-337` (`WriteRecipeMetadataAsync`) writes raw DB entity columns into both `recipe.info` and `recipe.json` (`backupContentVersion: 1`), while 9 unexposed `CurrentRecovery*` files (`CurrentRecoveryArchive`, `CurrentRecoveryRecipes`, `CurrentRecoveryScope`, `CurrentRecipeRecoveryAuthority`, `CurrentRecoveryHouseholdState*`, `ManagementService.CurrentRecovery*` — never exposed on `RecoveryController`) hardcode `wfs-recipe` v2 and `RecipeFilePublicationStore`.
  4. **Share portions & `SchemaOrgRecipe` fields**: `RecipeService.ExportRecipeShareBundle` (`286`) hardcodes `Servings = null` (mirrored in `pwa/e2e/mock-api.ts:1216`), `PrepareSharedSource` sets `Amount = null` on all imported lines, `BuildImportedRawMetadata` omits `@context` / `@type` / `recipeYield`, and `SchemaOrgRecipe.cs` lacks `Description` and `[JsonExtensionData]`.

## Decisions and Tradeoffs

1. **Pure `https://schema.org/Recipe` on disk (`{id}/recipe.json`) with lossless metadata preservation, derived `StructuredRecipeContent` in PostgreSQL**
   - **Decision**: `{id}/recipe.json` is always written as a top-level `SchemaOrgRecipe` JSON document (`@context: "https://schema.org/"`, `@type: "Recipe"`). Extend `SchemaOrgRecipe` (`api/src/RecipeApi/Models/SchemaOrgRecipe.cs`) with `Description` and `[JsonExtensionData]` so `nutrition`, `suggestedPairing`, `sourceName`, and any additional Schema.org properties in `RawMetadata` survive edits, backups, and startup unwraps intact; when structured ingredients/steps/servings are edited via `PATCH /api/recipes/{id}`, update `recipeYield` (preserving original phrasing when numeric basis still matches, otherwise `"<N> portions"` or `null`), `recipeIngredient`, `recipeInstructions` (`HowToSection` / `HowToStep`), and `supply` (`HowToSupply` entries synchronized from current lines). Move pure in-memory `StructuredRecipeContent` invariant validation (`ValidateContent` — unique GUIDs, provenance, UTF-16 step-binding spans) out of `RecipeExportContent.Current.cs` into `StructuredRecipeContentStore` now so Change 2 (`retire-transitional-recipe-wrapper-readers`) can delete `RecipeExportContent*` cleanly.
   - **Alternative & Tradeoff**: Keeping `wfs-recipe` v2 wrappers couples disk files to internal schema revisions; re-serializing `SchemaOrgRecipe` without `[JsonExtensionData]` would drop `sourceName` or extra Schema.org fields.

2. **One shared tolerant `SchemaOrgRecipe` → `StructuredRecipeContent` + bilingual `FoodId` builder across all intakes, restore, and startup**
   - **Decision**: Consolidate ingredient/instruction parsing into `StructuredRecipeContentStore` (reusing `RecipeStartupMigrator`'s EN/FR regex + `QuantityMath` parser, enhanced with optional unambiguous `SchemaOrgRecipe.Supply` name/unit/quantity hints, `CookingStepRelationshipProducer.Discover`, and `FoodMappingQualification.Prepare`). Update `FoodMappingQualification.SourceAligned` so lines with `Provenance.Origin == "source"` and `SourceRef == export.Content.SourceRevision` (without `"shopping"` in `ProtectedFields`) qualify against `FoodVocabularyAuthority` without requiring `originals[index] == Render(line)` or byte-identical `supply[index].name`. Unquantified or prose lines (`"Salt"`, `"Juice of 1 lemon"`) store `Amount = null` and `Description = trimmed` without throwing. Used identically by `DoSynthesizeRecipeAsync`, `DoExtractRecipeAsync` (fresh + guided repair), `ImportRecipeShareBundle`, `ManagementService.RestoreAsync`, and `RecipeStartupMigrator`.
   - **Alternative & Tradeoff**: Without updating `FoodMappingQualification.SourceAligned`, tolerant lines (`"30 ml of All-Purpose Flour"`, `"2 c. à soupe d'huile"`) would parse quantities yet silently drop bilingual `FoodId` links.

3. **Zero-reimport `v0.1.40` upgrade & independent disk unwrap in `RecipeStartupMigrator` (transitional readers retired in Change 2)**
   - **Decision**: Inject `RecipesRootResolver` / `IRecipeStore` into `RecipeStartupMigrator`:
     1. Upgrade any database recipe with `CanonicalContent == null` or `ContentSchemaVersion != 2` (all `v0.1.40` rows) offline in milliseconds using local `FoodVocabularyStore` and `QuantityMath` — **zero Gemini calls and zero recipe re-imports**.
     2. Independently scan `{RecipesRoot}/{id}/` for **all** recipe folders (even when DB `CanonicalContent` is already version 2): atomically unwrap any `backupContentVersion: 1` or `format: "wfs-recipe"` v2 `recipe.json` into top-level `https://schema.org/Recipe` JSON and delete any `.publications/` subdirectory.
     3. Retain a small transitional wrapper reader in `RecipeExportContent` for `ManagementService.RestoreAsync` and `SyncRecipeProcessor` during this release until `retire-transitional-recipe-wrapper-readers` removes it.
   - **Alternative & Tradeoff**: Tying disk unwrap to `NeedsMigration(dbRecipe)` would skip `./data/recipes/*/recipe.json` on installations whose PostgreSQL rows were already migrated. Running the disk scan independently guarantees 100% unwrap on startup.

4. **Complete subtraction of `RecipeFilePublicationStore`, launch-capture gates, `CanonicalBackup`, and unexposed `CurrentRecovery*` scaffolding**
   - **Decision**:
     - Delete `RecipeFilePublicationStore*.cs` (15 partials), `ManagementService.CanonicalBackup.cs` (`data/canonical-backup.json`), and the 9 unexposed `CurrentRecovery*` files (`CurrentRecoveryArchive`, `CurrentRecoveryRecipes`, `CurrentRecoveryScope`, `CurrentRecipeRecoveryAuthority`, `CurrentRecoveryHouseholdState*`, `ManagementService.CurrentRecovery*`).
     - Update `WorkflowWorker` (remove `HasExecutionAuthority`), `WorkflowOrchestrator` + `WorkflowOrchestrator.Launches` (launch workflows without `CaptureLaunchAsync` / `ValidateExecutionLaunch`), `WorkflowProcessorHistory` (validate task receipts without requiring `RecipeFilePublication[]`), `RecipeImportService`, `RecipeImportBulkService`, `RecipeImportReportService`, and `RecipeReportRepairIntent` (store/launch repair commands without `RecipePublicationCapture`).
     - Update all workflow processors and endpoints to read/write atomically via `IRecipeStore` / `RecipeRepository` and `RecipeDbContext` under `RecipeRotation.LockAsync` and `RecoveryBoundary`: `RecipeAgent` (`ExtractRecipe` including human-protected repair merge, `SynthesizeRecipe`, `GenerateDescription`), `RecipeHeroAgent`, `WebAcquisitionAgent`, `PdfConversionProcessor`, `PdfCaptureService`, `SyncRecipeProcessor` (synchronize/verify DB ↔ disk `SchemaOrgRecipe` + `recipe.info` and enlist/publish supper-projection invalidations), `CategorizeRecipeProcessor` (support `"goto-synthesis"` alongside `"url-import"` and `"recipe-import"`), `RecipeReadyProcessor` (persist `IsReady`, `IsDiscoverable`, `RotationSource`, `recipe.info`, duplicate check, and `recipe_ready` SSE), and `RecipeService.AddOriginalImageAsync` (`POST /api/recipes/{id}/originals`).
     - Fix all four `ManagementService` paths: `BackupAsync` writes pure `SchemaOrgRecipe` without `canonical-backup.json` or `RecipeFilePublications` busy checks; `RestoreAsync` allows merge updates on existing structured rows and derives `StructuredRecipeContent` + `FoodId` mappings (generating a fresh `contentRevision` so PostgreSQL's `guard_canonical_recipe_content` trigger is satisfied and `v0.1.40` backups become immediately portion-scalable without restart or re-import); `BackfillFinishedDishIndicesAsync` / `RestoreAsync` preserves `FinishedDishImageIndex` for structured recipes; and `DisasterRecoveryAsync` reads `recipe.info.AddedBy` first.
     - Update `RecoverySnapshotService` (`Capture`, `Restore`, `Demo`, `VocabularyRestore`): keep `recipe_file_publications` in `schema.sql` / `Tables` as an empty/inert table so Snapshot v3 and Demo v4 schema fingerprints remain compatible without destructive DDL, and update `WriteRecipeMetadataAsync` to write clean `RecipeInfo` to `recipe.info` and pure `https://schema.org/Recipe` to `recipe.json`.

5. **Rename internal `Canonical*` C# and PWA symbols to `StructuredRecipe*` without breaking wire or DB compatibility**
   - **Decision**: Rename `CanonicalRecipeContentStore*` → `StructuredRecipeContentStore*`, `CanonicalRecipeContent` → `StructuredRecipeContent`, `CanonicalRecipeOperation` → `StructuredRecipeOperation` (keeping `[Table("canonical_recipe_operations")]`), `CanonicalRecipeMigrationManifest` / `CanonicalRecipeMigrationReceipt*` → `StructuredRecipeMigration*` (keeping persisted `CommandType = "CanonicalRecipeCutoverReceipt"`), and `pwa/src/lib/api/canonical-edit-wire.ts` → `structured-edit-wire.ts` (`structuredIngredientLinesForTransport`), while keeping `[Column("canonical_content")]` and OpenAPI wire field names unchanged.

## First Observable Slice

1. Rename `CanonicalRecipe*` → `StructuredRecipe*`, implement `StructuredRecipeContentStore` (`FromSchemaOrg`, `ToSchemaOrg`, `ValidateContent`) + `FoodMappingQualification.SourceAligned` alignment, decouple `WorkflowOrchestrator.Launches`, `WorkflowProcessorHistory`, `WorkflowWorker`, `RecipeHeroAgent`, `SyncRecipeProcessor`, `CategorizeRecipeProcessor`, and `RecipeReadyProcessor` from `RecipeFilePublicationStore`, and run `POST /api/recipes/describe` (`goto-synthesis`) end-to-end — verifying pure `https://schema.org/Recipe` on disk, zero `.publications/` files, `IsReady == true`, bilingual `FoodId` resolution, and exact rational portion preview scaling.

## State, Concurrency and Recovery

- **PostgreSQL `guard_canonical_recipe_content` trigger invariant**: `api/database/schema.sql:461-477` enforces that once `recipes.canonical_content IS NOT NULL`, any update changing `source_url`, `raw_metadata`, `ingredients`, `servings`, or `canonical_content` MUST write a non-null `canonical_content` with a new `contentRevision`. Every writer (`EditAsync`, `DoExtractRecipeAsync` re-import/repair, `WebAcquisitionAgent` when `source_url` changes, `SyncRecipeProcessor`, `ManagementService.RestoreAsync`) either leaves those 5 columns untouched or writes a coherent updated `StructuredRecipeContent` with a fresh `contentRevision`.
- **Concurrent HTTP & worker mutations**: `RecoveryBoundary` (`RecoveryAccess.Write` vs. `Snapshot`/`Publish`), `RecipeRotation.LockAsync`, `WorkflowExecutionLease`, and `expectedContentRevision` optimistic concurrency protect human edits (`Provenance.Origin == "human"` / `ProtectedFields`) from background overwrites during extraction or guided import-report repair.

## Persistence and Side Effects

- **Authoritative disk artifacts**: `{id}/recipe.json` (pure `https://schema.org/Recipe` written atomically via `LocalStorageProvider.SaveAtomicallyAsync`) and `{id}/recipe.info` (`RecipeInfo`). No `.publications/` files or `data/canonical-backup.json` are written.
- **Share parity**: `ExportRecipeShareBundle` sets `Servings = recipe.Servings`; `ImportRecipeShareBundle` builds valid `https://schema.org/Recipe` `RawMetadata` (`@context`, `@type`, `recipeYield`, `totalTime`, `description`, `sourceName`), writes it to `{id}/recipe.json`, and derives `StructuredRecipeContent` + `FoodId` mappings; `pwa/src/lib/api/recipes.ts` and `pwa/e2e/mock-api.ts` preserve `servings`, `cuisineType`, and `mealTypes`.

## Compatibility, Rollout and Reversal

- **`dockerhub/v0.1.40` → latest upgrade**: `migrate.sh` (`compatibility.sql` + `sqldef`) adds all new columns/tables non-destructively; `FoodVocabularySeeder` + `RecipeStartupMigrator` convert all `v0.1.40` DB rows and unwrap all `backupContentVersion: 1` `recipe.json` files offline on first boot with zero AI calls and zero recipe re-imports; `WorkflowWorker` executes any pre-existing `v0.1.40` pending tasks cleanly.
- **Follow-up wrapper retirement**: Once deployed and after active backups/demo baselines are refreshed, `retire-transitional-recipe-wrapper-readers` deletes `RecipeExportContent*` and removes the startup wrapper-unwrap pass.
