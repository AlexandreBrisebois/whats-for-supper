# Proposal

## Why

Adding, importing, or restoring recipes currently fails or diverges from the durable `https://schema.org/Recipe` file standard introduced for long-term recipe portability:

1. **Broken AI synthesis, extraction, and bilingual food qualification**: `CanonicalRecipeContentStore.ConvertLines` (`api/src/RecipeApi/Services/CanonicalRecipeContentStore.cs`) enforces a strict, case-sensitive `EndsWith(" " + suffix, StringComparison.Ordinal)` check between `recipeIngredient` strings and `supply` entries, while `FoodMappingQualification.SourceAligned` (`api/src/RecipeApi/Services/FoodMappingQualification.cs`) skips bilingual `FoodId` matching unless `recipeIngredient[i] == Render(line)` and `supply[i].name == line.Shopping.Name` byte-for-byte. Valid `https://schema.org/Recipe` payloads produced by `synthesize-recipe.md`, `extract-recipe.md`, and `extract-web-recipe.md` (with capitalized `supply.name`, prepositions such as `of`/`de`/`d'`, unit aliases such as `c. à soupe` vs. `tablespoon`, or preparation wording such as `1 onion, diced`) fail with `InvalidDataException("Trusted legacy facts cannot be represented losslessly; conversion deferred.")` or silently lose grocery `FoodId` links. Description synthesis (`goto-synthesis`) crashes fatally; photo, PDF, and URL imports prematurely divert valid recipes into manual ingredient review; and `CategorizeRecipeProcessor` (`api/src/RecipeApi/Services/Processors/CategorizeRecipeProcessor.cs`) omits `"goto-synthesis"`, causing `RecipeReady` to fail with a field-hash conflict.
2. **Proprietary wrappers replaced `https://schema.org/Recipe` on disk, blocked `db-restore` merges, and lost portions on share**: `RecipeExportContent.Current.cs` writes `"format": "wfs-recipe", "version": 2` to `{id}/recipe.json` and `ManagementService.CanonicalBackup.cs` enforces `data/canonical-backup.json` `wfs-recipe` preflight while `ManagementService.RestoreAsync` rejects merge updates whenever `CanonicalContent != null`. Meanwhile, `v0.1.40` `db-backup` and `RecoverySnapshotService.Restore.cs` wrote `"backupContentVersion": 1` database-row dumps into `./data/recipes/*/recipe.json` (with the intact `https://schema.org/Recipe` string-escaped inside `rawMetadata`), `SchemaOrgRecipe.cs` omits `description` and `[JsonExtensionData]`, and `GET /api/recipes/{id}/share` (`api/src/RecipeApi/Services/RecipeService.cs`) sets `Servings = null` while `POST /api/recipes/import-bundle` imports shared lines with `Amount = null`.
3. **Unnecessary `.publications/` staging, workflow launch-authority locks, and leftover `Canonical*` naming**: Commit `3f36eb34` introduced `RecipeFilePublicationStore` (`.publications/` directories per recipe), `workflow_instances.recipe_publication_capture`, `WorkflowOrchestrator.Launches` / `WorkflowProcessorHistory` publication-receipt checks, `SyncRecipeProcessor` publication-row gates, and `WorkflowWorker.HasExecutionAuthority` (`ValidateExecutionLaunch`), causing `WorkflowWorker` to refuse pre-existing `v0.1.40` and restore-queued `Pending` tasks forever (`Workflow execution requires its original captured launch.`), even though household and demo capture/restore (`RecoverySnapshotService` + `RecoveryBoundary`) do not depend on per-recipe `.publications/` staging. Leftover `Canonical*` C#/PWA internal naming and unexposed `CurrentRecovery*` scaffolding also remained.

## Outcome and Scope

Restore pure `https://schema.org/Recipe` (`@context: "https://schema.org/"`, `@type: "Recipe"`) as the durable `{id}/recipe.json` disk representation, preserve recipe portions and bilingual grocery mappings across every capture/import/edit/share/backup/restore path, guarantee a zero-reimport upgrade from `dockerhub/v0.1.40` to the latest version, retire `.publications/` staging and workflow launch-authority locks, and replace internal `Canonical*` C#/PWA type and file naming with `StructuredRecipe*` naming.

Success is established when:

- **Zero recipe loss & zero-reimport upgrade from `v0.1.40`**: Upgrading from `dockerhub/v0.1.40` (or current builds) runs `RecipeStartupMigrator` offline before HTTP traffic or background workers start — deriving `servings`, exact rational `QuantityMath` amounts, step bindings, and local bilingual `FoodId` mappings in PostgreSQL without AI calls or recipe re-imports, atomically unwrapping all `backupContentVersion: 1` (all 198 files in `./data/recipes/*/recipe.json`) and `format: "wfs-recipe"` v2 `recipe.json` files on disk into pure top-level `https://schema.org/Recipe` JSON (even when PostgreSQL rows are already at version 2), and removing stale `.publications/` directories. Transitional readers continue to accept `backupContentVersion: 1` and `wfs-recipe` v2 during this release (retired in follow-up change `retire-transitional-recipe-wrapper-readers`).
- **Tolerant `https://schema.org/Recipe` intake across every capture, import, repair, and media path**: Description synthesis (`POST /api/recipes/describe` / `goto-synthesis`), photo capture (`POST /api/recipes` / `recipe-import`), PDF capture (`POST /api/recipes/capture-pdf` / `ConvertPdf` + `recipe-import`), URL capture (`POST /api/recipes/capture-url` / `FetchUrlContent` + `url-import`), share bundle import (`POST /api/recipes/import-bundle`), import-issue repair (`POST /api/recipes/{id}/import-report`), manual/bulk re-imports (`POST /api/recipes/imports/bulk`, `RecipeImportService`), failed-capture retry/clear (`POST /api/captures/failures/{id}/retry`, `DELETE /api/captures/failures/{id}`), original image upload (`POST /api/recipes/{id}/originals`), and hero/description regeneration (`POST /api/recipes/{id}/hero/regenerate`, `recipe-description-regeneration`) execute without `.publications/` staging or launch-authority refusals, write pure `https://schema.org/Recipe` to `{id}/recipe.json`, and derive rational `QuantityMath` amounts, step bindings, and bilingual `FoodId` mappings tolerantly.
- **Edits and sharing preserve `https://schema.org/Recipe` and portions**: Editing servings, structured ingredient lines, or instruction sections via `PATCH /api/recipes/{id}` updates PostgreSQL structured portion state (bumping `contentRevision` coherently with the `guard_canonical_recipe_content` trigger) and writes updated `recipeYield`, `recipeIngredient`, `supply`, and `recipeInstructions` to `{id}/recipe.json` as valid `https://schema.org/Recipe` while preserving retained metadata (`description`, `nutrition`, `suggestedPairing`, extension properties). `GET /api/recipes/{id}/share` exports saved `servings`, and `POST /api/recipes/import-bundle` preserves `servings` (`cuisineType`, `mealTypes`) and derives scalable portion lines on import.
- **Direct store persistence, unblocked workflows, and clean backup/restore/demo loops**: `RecipeFilePublicationStore`, `HasExecutionAuthority` / `ValidateExecutionLaunch`, `WorkflowOrchestrator.Launches` / `WorkflowProcessorHistory` publication-journal gates, `ManagementService.CanonicalBackup` (`data/canonical-backup.json`), and unexposed `CurrentRecovery*` `wfs-recipe` v2 scaffolding are retired. `ManagementService` (`db-backup`, `db-restore`, `db-disaster-recovery`) writes pure `https://schema.org/Recipe`, derives structured portion content during `db-restore` merge updates (so even restoring a `v0.1.40` backup works immediately without restart or re-import), and reads `recipe.info` first for member attribution. `RecoverySnapshotService` (`Capture`, `Restore`, `Demo`) preserves v3/v4 online exact recovery and demo reset while writing clean `RecipeInfo` to `recipe.info` and pure `https://schema.org/Recipe` to `recipe.json`.
- **Retire `Canonical*` internal naming**: Internal C# classes/files (`CanonicalRecipeContentStore*`, `CanonicalRecipeOperation`, `CanonicalRecipeMigrationManifest`, `CanonicalRecipeMigrationReceipt*`) and PWA helper `pwa/src/lib/api/canonical-edit-wire.ts` are renamed to `StructuredRecipe*` / `structured-edit-wire.ts`, while keeping OpenAPI wire property names and PostgreSQL table/column names (`[Column("canonical_content")]`, `[Table("canonical_recipe_operations")]`) compatible without destructive DDL.

Out of scope: Removing transitional `backupContentVersion: 1` and `wfs-recipe` v2 fallback readers (deferred to follow-up change `retire-transitional-recipe-wrapper-readers`), breaking OpenAPI wire contract changes, and altering `QuantityMath` arithmetic rules.

## What Changes

- Consolidate tolerant EN/FR `SchemaOrgRecipe` ↔ `StructuredRecipeContent` derivation and in-memory content validation inside `StructuredRecipeContentStore`, add `Description` and `[JsonExtensionData]` preservation to `SchemaOrgRecipe`, and align `FoodMappingQualification.Prepare` / `SourceAligned` so tolerant intake resolves bilingual `FoodId` mappings reliably.
- Write pure `https://schema.org/Recipe` (`@context: "https://schema.org/"`, `@type: "Recipe"`) to `{id}/recipe.json` across `RecipeAgent` (`ExtractRecipe`, `SynthesizeRecipe`, `GenerateDescription`), `RecipeService` (`DescribeRecipe`, `UpdateRecipe`, `ImportRecipeShareBundle`), `ManagementService` (`BackupAsync`), and `RecoverySnapshotService.Restore` (`WriteRecipeMetadataAsync`).
- Extend `RecipeStartupMigrator` with `RecipesRootResolver` / `IRecipeStore` to scan and atomically unwrap `backupContentVersion: 1` and `wfs-recipe` v2 `{id}/recipe.json` files on disk independently of DB migration status, delete `.publications/` residue, and upgrade `v0.1.40` recipes offline with zero AI calls or re-imports.
- Preserve `servings` on `GET /api/recipes/{id}/share` and derive structured portion lines + bilingual food mappings on `POST /api/recipes/import-bundle` (updating `pwa/e2e/mock-api.ts` and `pwa/src/lib/api/recipes.ts`).
- Retire `RecipeFilePublicationStore*`, `HasExecutionAuthority`, `UrlCaptureInitialization` / `RecipeFollowupInitialization` launch-hash gates, `WorkflowOrchestrator.Launches` / `WorkflowProcessorHistory` publication-journal checks, `RecipeReportRepairIntent` publication-capture coupling, `ManagementService.CanonicalBackup`, and unexposed `CurrentRecovery*` scaffolding; update `SyncRecipeProcessor`, `CategorizeRecipeProcessor`, `RecipeReadyProcessor`, `RecipeHeroAgent`, `WebAcquisitionAgent`, `PdfConversionProcessor`, `AddOriginalImageAsync`, and `ManagementService` (`RestoreAsync`, `BackfillFinishedDishIndicesAsync`, `DisasterRecoveryAsync`).
- Rename internal `CanonicalRecipe*` C# classes/files and `pwa/src/lib/api/canonical-edit-wire.ts` to `StructuredRecipe*` / `structured-edit-wire.ts`.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `recipe-capture`: All capture, extraction, description-synthesis, and guided repair paths accept valid `https://schema.org/Recipe` output tolerantly, persist pure `https://schema.org/Recipe` to `{id}/recipe.json` without `.publications/` staging, derive structured portion facts and bilingual food mappings, and complete `goto-synthesis` through categorization and readiness.
- `recipe-sharing`: Share bundle export preserves the recipe's saved `servings` basis, and share bundle import writes pure `https://schema.org/Recipe` to `{id}/recipe.json` and derives structured portion lines so imported shared recipes scale portions immediately.
- `recipe-editing`: Saving servings, structured ingredient lines, or instruction sections updates both structured database state and `{id}/recipe.json` in `https://schema.org/Recipe` format while preserving retained Schema.org metadata; internal canonical wording aligns with structured recipe content.
- `supper-servings`: Authoritative portion and grocery projections read structured recipe ingredient lines derived from `https://schema.org/Recipe` and edited recipe state, replacing legacy internal canonical terminology while keeping fail-closed v2 projection guarantees.
- `data-backup-and-recovery`: `{id}/recipe.json` uses `https://schema.org/Recipe` as the durable export/disk format, startup migration upgrades `v0.1.40` rows offline and unwraps existing `backupContentVersion: 1` and `wfs-recipe` v2 files in place while retaining transitional read support, merge restore updates and derives structured portion facts without rejecting migrated rows, and household/demo snapshot restore writes clean `RecipeInfo` + `https://schema.org/Recipe` without `.publications/` journals.
- `background-processing`: `WorkflowWorker` dispatches due `Pending` tasks under row locking, processor throttles, and `RecoveryBoundary` without refusing tasks for missing `recipe_publication_capture` launch-authority hashes.

## Impact

- **Backend (`api/src/RecipeApi/`)**: `StructuredRecipeContentStore*` (from `CanonicalRecipeContentStore*`), `FoodMappingQualification`, `SchemaOrgRecipe`, `RecipeAgent`, `RecipeHeroAgent`, `WebAcquisitionAgent`, `PdfCaptureService`, `PdfConversionProcessor`, `SyncRecipeProcessor`, `CategorizeRecipeProcessor`, `RecipeReadyProcessor`, `RecipeService`, `RecipeImportService`, `RecipeImportBulkService`, `RecipeImportReportService`, `RecipeReportRepairIntent`, `CaptureFailureService`, `WorkflowWorker`, `WorkflowOrchestrator*`, `WorkflowProcessorHistory`, `RecipeStartupMigrator`, `ManagementService`, `RecoverySnapshotService*`, `RecipeExportContent*`, and retirement of `RecipeFilePublicationStore*`, `ManagementService.CanonicalBackup`, and unexposed `CurrentRecovery*`.
- **Contract & Client (`specs/openapi.yaml`, `pwa/`)**: No breaking OpenAPI schema edits; update `pwa/src/lib/api/canonical-edit-wire.ts` → `structured-edit-wire.ts`, `pwa/src/lib/api/recipes.ts` (`cuisineType`/`mealTypes`/`servings` share parity), and `pwa/e2e/mock-api.ts` share/import handlers.
- **Data (`./data/recipes/`, PostgreSQL)**: Existing PostgreSQL tables/columns and `guard_canonical_recipe_content` trigger remain compatible without destructive DDL (`recipe_file_publications` stays empty/inert for Snapshot v3 / Demo v4 schema-hash compatibility); `v0.1.40` and `./data/recipes/*/recipe.json` upgrade cleanly in place with zero recipe re-imports and zero data loss.
