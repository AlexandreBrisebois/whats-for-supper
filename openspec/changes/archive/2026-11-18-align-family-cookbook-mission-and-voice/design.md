## Decisions and Tradeoffs

### 1. Retire "Solar Earth" -> `Shared Table` Identity + Contemporary Family Cookbook Aesthetic

- **Choice:** Replace `"Solar Earth"` with **`Shared Table`** across governance, skills, docs, SVG labels, and capability specs, while explicitly retaining **contemporary family cookbook** as the visual composition direction inside **`Shared Table`** (`"contemporary family cookbook within Shared Table"`, preserving the architectural pattern already established in [`docs/visual-language-reference.md`](../../../../docs/visual-language-reference.md#L43)).
- **Why:** **`Shared Table`** captures all four North Star anchors (_relief, togetherness, belonging, anticipation_) across the whole household experience—kids voting together, shaping the week, shopping in the grocery aisle, and choosing Take Out or a quick GOTO without guilt—while keeping **contemporary family cookbook** explicitly locked to food-forward photography, warm cream and white surfaces, confident Outfit dish titles, spacious readable ingredients, and soft layered depth so neither SaaS list density nor rustic parchment skeuomorphism creeps in.

### 2. Lean Single-Owner Split Between `mission.md` and `creative-direction-north-star.md`

- **Choice:** Following `.agents/core/mission.md`'s own subtraction and single-owner rules (§3), keep [`docs/creative-direction-north-star.md`](../../../../docs/creative-direction-north-star.md) intact except line 49 (`Keep Shared Table warm, distinctive and beautiful, with a contemporary family cookbook feel...`), and update [`.agents/core/mission.md`](../../../../.agents/core/mission.md) strictly in:
  - **§2 Product Intent:** Turn _"What's for supper?"_ from a dreaded daily question into a fun, collaborative household activity (**Swipe, Match, Cook!**) where everyone—kids included—helps choose the week's meals around recipes they love and a shared grocery list, leaving the household with one clear feeling: _"That’s one less thing weighing on me. We chose this together, it feels like us, and I’m looking forward to supper."_ Deliver this experience as a reliable, high-performance Progressive Web App (PWA) using a contract-first, test-driven approach.
  - **§4 Core Design & UX Patterns:** Update `"Solar Earth"` to warm, distinctive **`Shared Table`** design with a **contemporary family cookbook** aesthetic, pointing to the North Star for emotional direction.
- **Tradeoff:** Leaving speculative `"Four Household Energies"` taxonomy out of core repository governance avoids duplicating the North Star or prematurely locking experimental Track 4 shell concepts into permanent repo policy.

## First Observable Slice

The smallest behaviorally testable slice in this change is **Slice 2 + Slice 3**: updating `What's for Supper?` across `page.tsx` / `WelcomeForm.tsx` / `DemoExplanation.tsx` / `locales/`, updating `Synthesizing…` -> `capture.buildingRecipe` (plus `capture.buildingRecipeSubtext`, `capture.saveAndBuild`, and `familyGoto.describeDescription`) in `MinimalCapture.tsx` and locales, updating `'Recovery Flow'` -> `'Plan B'` fallback in `SkipRecoveryDialog.tsx`, removing `"Command Center"` / `"Matchmaking"` from `Header.tsx`, and adding `Vote Together` to `baseManifest.json`—exercised directly by `app-identity.test.ts`, `pdfManifest.test.ts`, `welcome/page.test.tsx`, `DemoContext.test.tsx`, `MinimalCapture.test.tsx`, `locale-integrity.test.ts`, and `install-identity.spec.ts`.

## Compatibility, Rollout and Reversal

- **Manifest shortcuts:** Additive entry in `baseManifest.json`; existing installed apps retain OS-cached metadata until refreshed (`app-installation` already notes no OS-cache guarantee).
- **Test impact:** Unit/E2E tests asserting the literal `'Whats for Supper?'` (`welcome/page.test.tsx`, `install-identity.spec.ts`, `welcome-demo.spec.ts`), `'Synthesizing…'` / `'Synthesize Recipe'` (`MinimalCapture.test.tsx`), or exact 2-item `shortcuts` array (`app-identity.test.ts`, `pdfManifest.test.ts`) must be updated in lockstep with the contract changes.
- **CSS alias preservation:** `.solar-earth-bg` and `SolarLoader` symbol renames wait until `establish-cookbook-design-system-and-visual-ratchet` so documentation naming moves cleanly ahead of code/CSS symbol migrations.

## User Experience

- Visitors opening Demo, Beta, Welcome, or `README.md` immediately understand both promises without dissonance: kids and household members get a fun, zero-friction way to vote on supper (**Swipe, Match, Cook!**), while the household coordinator sees calm reassurance (_"one less thing weighing on me"_).
- Eliminating `"Command Center"`, `"Matchmaking"`, and `"Synthesizing…"` while preserving warm idioms (`Table's Set!`, `Menu's In`, `Plan B`) removes technical and corporate friction during tired weekday moments while protecting the contemporary family cookbook look and feel on meal and recipe surfaces.
