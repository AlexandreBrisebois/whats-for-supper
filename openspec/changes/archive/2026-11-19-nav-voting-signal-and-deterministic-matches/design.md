# Design

## Context and Existing Ownership

Reference `proposal.md` for motivation and bounded scope, and the delta specifications under `specs/tonight-meal-decisions/spec.md`, `specs/recipe-discovery/spec.md`, and `specs/meal-planning/spec.md` for observable requirements.

Observed ownership across current source:

- **Voting session & navigation chrome:** `pwa/src/store/discoveryStore.ts` (`useDiscoveryStore`) owns the authoritative `VotingSessionSnapshot` (`phase`: `'unowned' | 'collecting' | 'converged'`, `roundId`, `weekStartDate`, `smartDefaults`), `sessionStatus`, and `hasPendingCards`. `pwa/src/hooks/useScheduleStream.ts` mounts once in `pwa/src/app/(app)/layout.tsx`, refreshes the session on SSE `open`, `pageshow`, and `visibilitychange`, and applies live `voting_session_updated` frames via `applySession`. `pwa/src/components/common/Navigation.tsx` reads `useDiscoveryStore`, calls `getCategories()` and `refreshSession()` on mount, renders the floating center medallion (`data-testid="nav-item-discovery"`), and applies `.animate-pulse-ochre` from `pwa/src/app/globals.css` only when `hasPendingCards && session?.phase === 'collecting' && !active`.
- **Home supper surface:** `pwa/src/components/home/HomeCommandCenter.tsx` currently subscribes to `useDiscoveryStore` solely to render `VotingNudgeCard` (`pwa/src/components/home/HomeSections.tsx`) below `TonightMenuCard` / `TonightPivotCard` / `CookedSuccessCard`, pushing voting prompts and `Checking voting…` / `Voting status unavailable.` below the fold on mobile viewports. `HomeSections.tsx` is the only consumer of the `home.votingRound`, `home.menuIsIn`, `home.familyVoting`, `home.matchesReady`, `home.checkingVoting`, `home.votingUnavailable*`, `home.seeMatches`, `home.voteNow`, and `home.retryVoting` locale strings.
- **Discovery celebration & Planner handoff:** `pwa/src/app/(app)/discovery/page.tsx` renders `SuccessCard` (`pwa/src/components/ui/SuccessCard.tsx`, cooled-herb `bg-garden-fill` to `bg-garden-fill-deep`) when `session?.phase === 'converged'`, routing `See your matches` via `usePlannerStore.getState().setActiveTab('planner')` to `/planner?weekOffset=0&referenceWeekStartDate=<monday>`. `Navigation.tsx` already invokes `requestPortionsDeparture` (`pwa/src/lib/planner/portionsNavigation.ts`) before navigation.
- **Planner voting action row & header signal:** `pwa/src/store/weekStore.ts` (`useWeekStore`) owns `status` (`0 | 1 | 2`), `weekStartDate`, `votingRoundId`, `schedule` (`_isPending` recipe suggestions), and `closeVoting()` (which promotes non-skipped `_isPending` slots via `assignRecipeToDay` and locks the round via `lockSchedule`). In `pwa/src/app/(app)/planner/page.tsx`, lines `816-821` render an `animate-ping bg-ochre-600` dot whenever `isVotingOpen`, lines `925-971` render `planner-action-row` containing solid green `nudge-family-cta` (`Share2`) and terracotta-outlined `close-voting-btn` (`Ban`, `"Close Voting"` / `"Fermer le vote"`), and lines `1074-1085` render the post-close `planner-week-finalized` toast (`planner.weekFinalized`, currently `"Menu's in!"` in English and `"C'est validé !"` in French).
- **Household-wide consensus placement:** `api/src/RecipeApi/Services/SmartDefaultsReader.cs` (`SmartDefaultsReader.ReadAsync`) computes `PreSelectedRecipes` from household-wide `RecipeVotes`, `FamilyMembers`, and `CalendarEvents` (no per-member filter), and `api/src/RecipeApi/Services/VotingSession.cs` broadcasts that single `VotingSessionSnapshot` via SSE. However, `SmartDefaultsReader.cs` orders candidates by `.OrderByDescending(x => x.VoteCount == familySize).ThenByDescending(x => x.Recipe.LastCookedDate)` without a tie-breaker—unlike `DiscoveryService.GetRecipesForDiscoveryAsync` (`api/src/RecipeApi/Services/DiscoveryService.cs`) and `ScheduleService.FillTheGapAsync` (`api/src/RecipeApi/Services/ScheduleService.cs`), which both terminate in ascending `RecipeId` ordering.

## Decisions and Tradeoffs

1. **Remove `VotingNudgeCard` from Home instead of keeping a condensed banner:**
   - *Decision:* Delete `VotingNudgeCard` and Home's voting-status/retry UI from `HomeCommandCenter.tsx` and `HomeSections.tsx`.
   - *Alternative & tradeoff:* Shrinking `VotingNudgeCard` or moving it above `TonightMenuCard` would either remain below the fold or compete directly with Tonight's dinner and `Capture a Recipe`. Subtraction keeps Home focused on tonight's supper while trusting the persistent center nav medallion (always above the fold on all routes) to carry voting status.
2. **State-bound persistent Garden green vs. short-lived green flash back to yellow:**
   - *Decision:* Keep the center medallion in steady Garden green (`bg-garden-fill text-white border-garden-fill-deep`) with `Check` and `"Menu's in"` / `"Menu prêt"` for the entire `converged` phase until `Close Voting` returns the session to `unowned` or a new `collecting` round starts.
   - *Alternative & tradeoff:* A brief green flash that reverts to yellow would be missed by interrupted household members who were not watching the screen when convergence occurred, and returning to yellow while `converged` contradicts `/discovery` still holding `SuccessCard` until voting closes. Keeping it state-bound matches real lifecycle truth.
3. **Direct-to-Planner converged navigation vs. routing through `/discovery`:**
   - *Decision:* When `session.phase === 'converged'` with a valid `roundId` and `absoluteMonday(session.weekStartDate)`, tapping `nav-item-discovery` sets `usePlannerStore.getState().setActiveTab('planner')` and navigates directly to `/planner?weekOffset=0&referenceWeekStartDate=<monday>` (honoring `requestPortionsDeparture` and falling back to `/discovery` if no valid Monday exists). `/discovery` retains `SuccessCard` for in-progress voters and direct `/discovery` links (e.g., `Nudge family` shares).
   - *Alternative & tradeoff:* Sending converged nav taps to `/discovery` would force every household member through a redundant second *"Menu's in!"* screen and second tap. Direct routing trades a one-line tab title alignment (Planner lands cleanly on the Meals tab with pending suggestions and `Close Voting`) for a 1-tap path from any screen.
4. **Stateful label `"Menu's in"` / `"Menu prêt"` vs. fixed `"Discovery"` / `"Découverte"`:**
   - *Decision:* Display `"Menu's in"` (EN) and `"Menu prêt"` (FR) under the green `Check` medallion during `converged`, using `text-garden-ink` (`>= 12px` `text-xs font-semibold`). Both strings are 9 characters, matching `"Discovery"` (9) and `"Découverte"` (10) in width so narrow `320px` and `200%`-scaled layouts stay balanced.
   - *Alternative & tradeoff:* A fixed `"Discovery"` label improves pure spatial muscle memory, whereas `"Menu's in"` / `"Menu prêt"` eliminates ambiguity on first convergence without needing a tooltip or Home card. Keep `data-testid="nav-item-discovery"` unchanged so navigation selectors remain stable.
5. **Deterministic `.ThenBy(x => x.RecipeId)` in `SmartDefaultsReader.cs`:**
   - *Decision:* Append `.ThenBy(x => x.RecipeId)` to the `filteredVotes` ordering in `SmartDefaultsReader.ReadAsync`.
   - *Alternative & tradeoff:* Sorting by recipe name could change on edits or produce case/collation differences across EF/InMemory/PostgreSQL, whereas `Guid` `RecipeId` ascending matches existing tie-breaking in `DiscoveryService.cs` and `ScheduleService.FillTheGapAsync` with zero OpenAPI or database schema changes. Late votes before `Close Voting` still trigger live SSE recalculations via `VotingSession` as designed.
6. **Approach 1 in Planner — Promote `close-voting-btn` and hide `Nudge family` only on confirmed household convergence:**
   - *Decision:* In `pwa/src/app/(app)/planner/page.tsx`, compute `isConvergedWeek` strictly from confirmed household-wide convergence for the loaded round and week (`isVotingOpen && session?.phase === 'converged' && Boolean(votingRoundId && session.roundId === votingRoundId) && absoluteMonday(session.weekStartDate) === weekStartDate`), and never from `hasPendingCards === false` (another household member may still have cards left while `collecting`). When `isConvergedWeek` is true (both in ordinary Planner and in Portions mode), hide `nudge-family-cta`—since nothing remains to vote for—freeing full row width beside the `Portions` chip for `close-voting-btn`, which upgrades from terracotta `<Ban />` to primary solid Garden green (`bg-garden-fill hover:bg-garden-fill-deep text-white`) with `<Check />`. When `hasPendingSuggestions` is true, label it `"Save menu & close voting"` / `"Enregistrer et fermer le vote"`; when zero pending suggestions exist (e.g., all 7 slots already occupied), label it `"Close Voting"` / `"Fermer le vote"` so the label never claims unsaved matches exist when none do. Also switch the week-header voting dot to steady Garden green when `isConvergedWeek` (with `motion-reduce:animate-none` on the collecting ping), and change EN `planner.weekFinalized` from `"Menu's in!"` to `"Menu saved!"` (keeping FR `"C'est validé !"`) so `"Menu's in"` means converged-awaiting-review while `"Menu saved!"` confirms persisted closure.
   - *Alternative & tradeoff:* Keeping a secondary outlined `Nudge family` or adding an above-Monday banner (Approach 2) / sticky bottom bar (Approach 3) either crowds the single action row beside `Portions` or adds vertical height and extra surface state. Approach 1 with conditional hiding of `Nudge family` reuses the existing `closeVoting()` persistence boundary (`data-testid="close-voting-btn"`), keeps height zero-growth on open, and completes a coherent color narrative from green `Menu's in` bottom nav -> green `Save menu & close voting` action -> toast `"Menu saved!"` + quiet ochre `Discovery` medallion.

## First Observable Slice

The smallest end-to-end slice proving household consistency is **Slice 1** (`SmartDefaultsReader.cs`): seeding two or more consensus recipes with identical unanimity and null `LastCookedDate` (including more tied candidates than open slots) and asserting via `ScheduleServiceTests.cs` (and `VotingSessionTests.cs`) that repeated `GetSmartDefaultsAsync` and `VotingSession.ReadAsync` calls select identical `PreSelectedRecipes` ordered by ascending `RecipeId` onto identical `DayIndex` slots. Subsequent slices build the 3-state `Navigation` medallion with reduced motion (Slice 2), remove Home's `VotingNudgeCard` (Slice 3), promote Planner's converged save-and-close action row and steady green header dot (Slice 4), and verify full browser/a11y/docs integration (Slice 5).

## State, Concurrency and Recovery

- **Three-state medallion projection in `Navigation.tsx`:**
  - `converged`: `session?.phase === 'converged'` and `Boolean(session.roundId)` and `Boolean(absoluteMonday(session.weekStartDate))` -> Garden green `Check`, `"Menu's in"` / `"Menu prêt"`, target `/planner?weekOffset=0&referenceWeekStartDate=<monday>`. (If `session?.phase === 'converged'` lacks a valid Monday, render the steady green `Check` or fallback safely to `/discovery` without guessing a week.)
  - `collecting`: `hasPendingCards && session?.phase === 'collecting' && pathname !== ROUTES.DISCOVERY` -> Sunlight ochre `Compass`, `"Discovery"` / `"Découverte"`, `.animate-pulse-ochre` + static status pip, target `/discovery`.
  - `unowned` / unavailable / active on `/discovery` / no remaining cards: static Sunlight ochre `Compass`, `"Discovery"` / `"Découverte"`, no pulse and no pip, target `/discovery`.
- **Converged Planner derivation & safe fallback in `pwa/src/app/(app)/planner/page.tsx`:**
  - `weekStartDate` and `votingRoundId` come from `useWeekStore`, and `session` comes from `useDiscoveryStore`.
  - `isConvergedWeek = isVotingOpen && session?.phase === 'converged' && Boolean(votingRoundId && session.roundId === votingRoundId) && absoluteMonday(session.weekStartDate) === weekStartDate`.
  - `hasPendingSuggestions = schedule.some((day) => day.status !== 3 && day._isPending && Boolean(day.recipe?.id))`.
  - When `session` is loading, unavailable, in `collecting`, or belongs to a different round/week, `isConvergedWeek` evaluates to `false` and Planner falls back cleanly to standard `collecting` presentation (`Nudge family` + terracotta-outlined `Close Voting` + ochre collecting dot), never prematurely hiding `Nudge family` just because the current member has 0 remaining cards (`hasPendingCards === false`).
- **Single-shot arrival cue without replay:** Track the previous observed `session?.phase` (and `roundId`) in a ref inside `Navigation.tsx` so transitioning into `converged` from `collecting` or `unowned` may play one calm CSS arrival animation (omitted under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`), while initial hydration into `converged`, route transitions, or subsequent `revision` updates within the same `converged` round remain completely static.
- **Stale session & departure guards:** Authoritative revision ordering, host-incarnation reconciliation, and reconnect/resume synchronization remain owned by `useDiscoveryStore.applySession` and `useScheduleStream.ts`. Activating the converged medallion sets `usePlannerStore.getState().setActiveTab('planner')` inside the navigation continuation callback only after `requestPortionsDeparture` permits departure (or immediately when no Portions draft blocks navigation), preventing tab switches if the user cancels leaving an unsaved Portions edit. In Planner, `close-voting-btn` continues invoking `handleCloseVoting`, which promotes non-skipped `_isPending` suggestions and calls `lockSchedule` with existing round/week mismatch recovery and Portions draft protections intact.
- **Landmark semantics:** Keep `aria-current={active ? 'page' : undefined}` bound to `/discovery` (when `href === ROUTES.DISCOVERY && pathname === ROUTES.DISCOVERY`), so landing on `/planner` via the converged medallion highlights the Planner tab as `aria-current="page"` rather than duplicating `aria-current="page"` on two nav items.

## User Experience

- **Era 3 tokens & contrast (Standard AA + High Contrast AAA):**
  - `unowned` & `collecting` medallion: `bg-ochre-100 text-sunlight-ink border-sunlight-line` (`#856400` on `#FFF7CC` = `5.09:1` AA; `#664E01` on `#FFF7CC` = `7.32:1` AAA) with `text-sunlight-ink` label. The `collecting` static status pip uses `bg-sunlight-ink` with a `ring-2 ring-cream-100` separator on the top-right rim of the orb (`>= 3:1` non-text contrast in both modes, `aria-hidden="true"`).
  - `converged` medallion & Planner close CTA: `bg-garden-fill text-white` (`#4E6E52` + `#FFFFFF` = `5.71:1` AA; `#324D36` + `#FFFFFF` = `9.33:1` AAA; hover `bg-garden-fill-deep` `#3D5940` AA / `#243A27` AAA) with crisp white `Check` icon (`aria-hidden="true"`), and `text-garden-ink` nav label (`#4E6E52` on `#FDFCF0` = `5.44:1` AA; `#324D36` AAA).
- **Accessible names, motion, and targets:**
  - `unowned` nav uses the visible `"Discovery"` / `"Découverte"` label directly so existing default link semantics match.
  - `collecting` nav exposes a descriptive localized `aria-label` (e.g., `"Discovery — family is voting"` / `"Découverte — la famille vote"`).
  - `converged` nav exposes a descriptive localized `aria-label` (e.g., `"Menu's in — open meal plan matches"` / `"Menu prêt — voir les choix du menu"`).
  - Planner converged `close-voting-btn` uses `min-h-12 min-w-12 py-2 px-4 rounded-full text-xs font-semibold text-center leading-tight` (without single-line truncation or fixed height that could clip) so longer strings (`"Save menu & close voting"` / `"Enregistrer et fermer le vote"`) wrap cleanly beside `Portions` across `320px`, `200%` text scaling, `390x844`, `412x915`, and `820x1180` viewports with zero horizontal overflow.
  - Planner week-header dot renders steady `bg-garden-fill` without `animate-ping` when `isConvergedWeek`; the `collecting` `animate-ping` span includes `motion-reduce:animate-none` while keeping its static `bg-ochre-600` dot.
  - `globals.css` adds `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` rules disabling `.animate-pulse-ochre` and any arrival animation, while the static `collecting` pip and `converged` `Check` icon + label preserve complete meaning without motion or hue alone.
  - Nav preserves `min-h-12 min-w-12` (`>= 48 x 48 CSS px`), `focus-visible:outline-2 focus-visible:outline-offset-4 focus-visible:outline-ink-body`, and zero horizontal overflow across all tested viewports in EN and FR.
