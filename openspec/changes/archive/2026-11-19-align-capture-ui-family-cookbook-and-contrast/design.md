## Context and Existing Ownership

Observed current implementation across Capture shells, active components, and tests:

- **Capture shell & sticky header:** `pwa/src/app/(app)/layout.tsx` renders `<Header />` exclusively for `/capture` (`hideHeader` is `true` for Home, Recipes, Planner, Discovery, Profile, and Settings). `usesStackBackground` excludes `isCapture`, the `capture-cancel-btn` link uses `text-charcoal-600`, `transition-all`, unlocalized `aria-label="Cancel capture"`, and no `focus-visible` ring, and `t('capture.addRecipe', ...)` reads static `t()` without `useLocale()`. [Header.tsx](../../../../pwa/src/components/common/Header.tsx) hardcodes `bg-cream/90 backdrop-blur-xl border-b border-terracotta/5` and `text-charcoal`. `pwa/src/app/(app)/capture/page.tsx` caps the column at `max-w-sm` (`384px`, narrower than Home `max-w-[27rem]` / Profile `max-w-md`) and uses `bg-charcoal/5` for its suspense skeleton.
- **Main Capture wizard (`MinimalCapture.tsx`):** [MinimalCapture.tsx](../../../../pwa/src/components/capture/MinimalCapture.tsx) mixes pre-semantic static tokens with Standard-AA failures (`text-charcoal/50`, `text-charcoal-300`, `text-ochre-600`), zero `--role-*` semantic variables, an explicit override on `<Button variant="primary">` back to static `bg-terracotta` (breaking High Contrast AAA), translucent `bg-cream/90 backdrop-blur-md` overlays and faint alpha borders (`border-charcoal/5`, `border-charcoal/8`, `border-charcoal/10`, `border-terracotta/10`), sub-48px buttons (`Pick from Gallery`, `View existing recipe`, `h-6 w-6` remove-photo), `<div onClick>` photo tiles, missing `type="button"` / `aria-pressed` on ratings, unassociated `<label>` tags, missing `role="alert"` on validation errors, unlocalized strings without `useLocale()`, `uppercase tracking-widest` / `font-black` typography, `Loader2` spinners instead of [CookbookLoader](../../../../pwa/src/components/ui/SolarLoader.tsx), unguarded `animate-ping`/`animate-pulse`/`shake`/`zoom-in`, and `URL.createObjectURL(file)` called inside `.map()` render without `URL.revokeObjectURL`.
- **PDF & global Capture notifications:** [PdfCaptureConfirmation.tsx](../../../../pwa/src/components/capture/PdfCaptureConfirmation.tsx) and [PdfCapturePanel.tsx](../../../../pwa/src/components/capture/PdfCapturePanel.tsx) use static `charcoal/*`, `terracotta-600/700`, `font-black`, uniform terracotta selection on all three ratings, `focus:outline-none`, and `t(...)` without `useLocale()`. [LibraryToast.tsx](../../../../pwa/src/components/capture/LibraryToast.tsx) and [RecipeFailureBanner.tsx](../../../../pwa/src/components/capture/RecipeFailureBanner.tsx) (plus [RecipeFailureBanner.test.tsx](../../../../pwa/src/components/capture/RecipeFailureBanner.test.tsx#L47)) hardcode olive `sage-*`, translucent `bg-white/95 backdrop-blur-md`, static `terracotta*`/`charcoal*`, English strings, and Framer Motion springs without `useReducedMotion()`.
- **Unused legacy artifacts & E2E gap:** `pwa/src/app/(app)/capture/confirm/page.tsx` is a Phase 0 placeholder (`ROUTES.CAPTURE_CONFIRM` in [routes.ts](../../../../pwa/src/lib/constants/routes.ts#L8)), and `pwa/src/components/capture/index.ts` exports five unused legacy components (`CameraView.tsx`, `ImageReview.tsx` + `ImageReview.test.tsx`, `NotesField.tsx`, `RatingSelector.tsx`, `SubmitConfirmation.tsx`). [capture-flow.spec.ts](../../../../pwa/e2e/capture-flow.spec.ts), [pdf-capture.spec.ts](../../../../pwa/e2e/pdf-capture.spec.ts), and [url-capture-recovery.spec.ts](../../../../pwa/e2e/url-capture-recovery.spec.ts) do not invoke [assertCookbookVisualA11y](../../../../pwa/e2e/visual-a11y-contract.ts).

## Decisions and Tradeoffs

1. **Shell header parity (`.glass-header` in `globals.css`) over ad-hoc component overrides:**
   - **Choice:** Mirror the `.glass-nav` pattern in [globals.css](../../../../pwa/src/app/globals.css) with a `.glass-header` utility (`--role-nav-bg`, crisp `border-chrome-line` in High Contrast, `backdrop-filter: none` under `:root[data-contrast="high"]`) used by [Header.tsx](../../../../pwa/src/components/common/Header.tsx), and enable `.cookbook-bg` for `isCapture` in `(app)/layout.tsx` alongside `max-w-md` container width in `capture/page.tsx`.
   - **Alternative rejected:** Making `Header.tsx` unconditionally opaque in both modes throws away calm frosted depth in Standard AA; tying it to `[data-contrast="high"]` matches bottom navigation behavior uniformly.

2. **Full migration to dual-mode semantic `--role-*` tokens & `<Button>` primitives:**
   - **Choice:** Remove the static `bg-terracotta` override on `<Button variant="primary">` at [MinimalCapture.tsx](../../../../pwa/src/components/capture/MinimalCapture.tsx#L1522), reuse `<Button>` (`primary`, `secondary`, `ghost`, `danger`) across Capture actions, and map every remaining custom button, surface, border, alert, badge, and overlay onto semantic tokens (`text-ink-body`, `text-ink-muted`, `bg-hearth-fill`/`hover`/`active`, `text-hearth-ink`, `border-hearth-line`, `bg-garden-fill`, `text-garden-ink`, `border-garden-line`, `text-sunlight-ink`, `border-sunlight-line`, `border-chrome-line`, `text-icon-affordance`). Full-screen upload overlays use `bg-cream/95` with `backdrop-blur-md` in Standard mode and opaque `bg-cream` (`[data-contrast=high]`) via CSS rule rather than translucent washout.
   - **Rating polarity & non-colour cues:** Match [RecipeDetailSheet.tsx](../../../../pwa/src/components/recipes/RecipeDetailSheet.tsx#L2211-L2245)'s semantic polarity (`1` → `text-hearth-ink`, `2` → `text-garden-ink`, `3` → `text-ink-body`, tonal/white selected plate, `type="button"`, `aria-pressed={isSelected}`, explicit non-colour check indicator, `min-h-12`, `focus-visible:outline-2 focus-visible:outline-offset-2 focus-visible:outline-ink-body`) while preserving existing `capture-rating-{1..3}` and `pdf-rating-{1..3}` `data-testid` attributes.

3. **Touch geometry & thumbnail preview buttons without sacrificing compact cards:**
   - **Choice:** Give `Pick from Gallery` (`min-h-12 w-full`), `View existing recipe` (`min-h-12 inline-flex items-center px-3`), `Clear All`, `Back`, and toast/banner actions explicit `>= 48 × 48 CSS px` interactive geometry. Convert staged photo tiles into `<button type="button">` preview triggers paired with an accessible `h-12 w-12` (`48px`) remove-photo `<button>` (avoiding nested interactive `<button>` elements by keeping the preview button and remove button sibling controls inside a `relative` wrapper).
   - **Alternative rejected:** Expanding the photo remove `×` visually inside a tiny `3:4` corner covers too much photo preview; using a transparent/well-padded `h-12 w-12` hit target with a centered visual badge or clear sibling control keeps >= 48px tap safety cleanly.

4. **Deterministic object-URL lifecycle for staged photos:**
   - **Choice:** Replace inline `URL.createObjectURL(file)` during JSX `.map()` in `MinimalCapture.tsx` with a keyed `WeakMap<File, string>` or ref-backed `Map<File, string>` reconciled inside `useEffect` whenever `photos` changes and on unmount, calling `URL.revokeObjectURL` for removed files and remaining entries on unmount. Verify creation and revocation in [MinimalCapture.test.tsx](../../../../pwa/src/components/capture/MinimalCapture.test.tsx).

5. **Retire dead Capture stub/wrappers rather than spending migration effort on unreachable code:**
   - **Choice:** Delete `pwa/src/app/(app)/capture/confirm/page.tsx`, remove unused `ROUTES.CAPTURE_CONFIRM` from [routes.ts](../../../../pwa/src/lib/constants/routes.ts#L8), and remove `CameraView.tsx`, `ImageReview.tsx`, `ImageReview.test.tsx`, `NotesField.tsx`, `RatingSelector.tsx`, `SubmitConfirmation.tsx`, and `pwa/src/components/capture/index.ts`. Object-URL lifecycle unit assertions move to `MinimalCapture.test.tsx`.
   - **Verified precondition:** Repository-wide `grep` confirmed zero active route, test, or component consumers of those files outside their own folder and barrel export.

6. **Localization reactivity & reduced motion:**
   - **Choice:** Read `const { locale } = useLocale()` in `(app)/layout.tsx`, `MinimalCapture.tsx`, `PdfCaptureConfirmation.tsx`, `PdfCapturePanel.tsx`, `LibraryToast.tsx`, and `RecipeFailureBanner.tsx`; add missing `capture.*` / `notifications.*` keys cleanly to both [en/common.json](../../../../pwa/src/locales/en/common.json) and [fr/common.json](../../../../pwa/src/locales/fr/common.json) in sentence case (replacing `uppercase tracking-widest` button treatments). Wrap Tailwind `animate-ping`/`animate-pulse`/`zoom-in`/`shake` utilities with `motion-reduce:animate-none` and guard Framer Motion transitions in `LibraryToast` / `RecipeFailureBanner` with `useReducedMotion()`.

## First Observable Slice

Begin with shell chrome & shared token parity (`globals.css` `.glass-header`, `(app)/layout.tsx`, `Header.tsx`, `capture/page.tsx`) plus `RecipeFailureBanner` and `LibraryToast`, verified via component tests cleanly updating [RecipeFailureBanner.test.tsx](../../../../pwa/src/components/capture/RecipeFailureBanner.test.tsx#L47). Next, migrate `MinimalCapture.tsx`, `PdfCaptureConfirmation.tsx`, `PdfCapturePanel.tsx`, and EN/FR locales (including `useLocale()`, object-URL management, associated `<label>`/`htmlFor`, `role="alert"`, `>= 48px` targets, and `<CookbookLoader />`), retire the dead stub/wrapper files, and extend [capture-flow.spec.ts](../../../../pwa/e2e/capture-flow.spec.ts), [pdf-capture.spec.ts](../../../../pwa/e2e/pdf-capture.spec.ts), and [url-capture-recovery.spec.ts](../../../../pwa/e2e/url-capture-recovery.spec.ts) with `assertCookbookVisualA11y` in both `'aa'` and `'aaa'` modes.

## User Experience

- **Standard (`standard`) vs. High Contrast (`high`):** Standard AA keeps warm cream/paper lightness, calm `--role-*` accent hierarchy (`#B85542` primary, `#A44A37` Hearth ink, `#4E6E52` Garden ink, `#856400` Sunlight ink, `#6E645B` muted ink), and subtle translucent sticky header chrome. Switching the member or OS setting to High Contrast immediately snaps Capture headers, cards, overlays, failure banners, completion toasts, inputs, and buttons to `>= 7.0:1` AAA inks, opaque cream/white chrome, and crisp `chrome-line` / `hearth-line` / `garden-line` borders without horizontal overflow at `320px` or `200%` text scaling.
- **Keyboard & screen-reader behavior:** Every interactive control exposes native button/link semantics (`type="button"`), associated `<label htmlFor>` bindings, `aria-pressed` + non-colour check cues on ratings, `role="alert"` on errors/warnings, and explicit `focus-visible` outlines.
