# Design

## Context and Existing Ownership

`RecipesExperience` supplies the current Browse or Search summary to `RecipeStackCard` and keeps collection, filter, planner and detail identity. `RecipeStackCard` owns the draggable visual face; its 16:10 hero, scrollable/faded description and bottom time row put the time at the clipped edge in Alex's 912×2048 screenshot. `StackActionBar` owns position and Ask the Family outside the card. `RecipesExperience` currently renders Previous/Next buttons only for Search; Explore depends on swiping, and detail entry depends on tapping the front card. A stationary front-card tap opens `RecipeDetailSheet`; the sheet loads complete recipe detail by identity and remains unchanged.

`HomeCommandCenter` passes `currentRecipe.totalTime || undefined` to `TonightMenuCard`. Its default `totalTime = '30-45 mins'` becomes `30 MINS` in `formatRecipeTime`, so missing source data appears as a factual estimate. The formatter also serves stack, detail, Recipes list, Discovery and Quick Find. Detail currently renders an empty-time Ready In label under its own explicit contract. The Home badge is a terracotta-blush clock pill above the photo; detail uses a smaller white clock pill below it; stack currently has an ochre pill without a clock below its photo.

## Decisions and Tradeoffs

1. **Keep the card face simple.** Use the hero and a complete wrapping name, with only a time pill over the image when formatting yields a nonempty duration. Remove the description's nested scrolling and fade from the face. A long name must grow in normal layout rather than hide behind the photo or time. Keep the photo dominant at ordinary phone size; constrain its height only when the available viewport is genuinely short. Retain the existing card width bound and four-card depth.
2. **Reuse the time language, not a new time rule.** Use `formatRecipeTime(totalTime)` and `common.readyIn` in both cards. Borrow Home's Clock icon and warm terracotta-blush pill for the stack; over arbitrary food pixels use an opaque light backing and dark existing terracotta/charcoal text, with a visible edge or shadow. Position it away from the primary food subject when possible. The returned value is total time, never preparation-only time. Do not modify the formatter or introduce a shared component just to deduplicate two small views.
3. **Treat unavailable time as unavailable.** Render either badge only when the formatted value is nonempty. Remove Home's default `'30-45 mins'`; do not change valid ISO output or other duration consumers. This also avoids an empty `READY IN` badge for a malformed but nonempty value on these two surfaces. The detail sheet's explicit empty-label behavior stays intact in this change.
4. **Restore the existing interaction model.** Remove the newly introduced visible Previous / View recipe / Next row. The front card remains the detail entry by stationary tap or Enter/Space and the navigation surface by horizontal drag. Retain any Search-specific boundary/navigation controls from the pre-correction unified flow without adding a new row to Explore. `handleSwipeRight`, `handleSwipeLeft` and `handleCardTap` remain the collection/detail owners, so Explore wrap, Search cursor/end behavior, pending requests, identity and planner target do not change. Keep visible focus on the front card and restore focus there when its detail sheet closes if it is still mounted.
5. **Align backing cards to the front face.** Let the front card's image and complete wrapping name establish the stack's intrinsic height. Do not stretch the face to consume an otherwise empty result region. Bound every absolute backing card to that front-card box and clip its own hidden content; a longer name on a backing record must not enlarge the back card. Preserve reduced scale/opacity and only small, consistent vertical peeks, with an explicit bottom-edge comparison in rendered tests. Keep the full front name visible at enlarged text and let the outer result region scroll above the dock when needed.
6. **Preserve status outside the face.** The existing import-issue badge must remain discoverable when returned, but its current photo overlay conflicts with Alex's image/name/time-only face; place it immediately adjacent to the card or in the existing action region, with its current meaning and detail path. Keep position and Ask the Family external. Do not alter swipe thresholds or tap suppression.

## First Observable Slice

Open `/recipes` in stack mode with a front long name, a longer backing-card name, description and `PT1H30M`. Through the existing route and GET or POST summary seam, the first card presents its hero, full name and localized `1H 30 MINS` badge over the photo, without description on the face. The backing card stays within the front card's measured box apart from its small intentional depth offset. Tapping or pressing Enter/Space on the front card opens the same detail sheet and returns focus on dismissal; horizontal drag navigates under the existing collection rules. No new visible three-button row appears. Subsequent checks cover Search continuation/exhaustion, Explore wrap, absent/unformattable time, issue status and the Home route's schedule-to-card value handoff. No API request or schema changes are needed.

## User Experience

The stack remains a warm, photo-led cookbook surface: cream canvas, soft rounded white card, Outfit title, restrained terracotta time pill and shallow aligned depth. The pill must remain readable over light and dark photos without relying on translucent photo pixels. Its Clock icon supplements the localized text; no essential meaning is conveyed by color alone. At 320×568 and 390×667, the initial view must expose a recognizable image and complete title when the toolbar is collapsed. If Search controls or planner context consume that space, the card and existing actions remain reachable by intentional scrolling. At 667×375, on iPad and at 200% text in English or French, content can scroll and reflow without clipped names or dock overlap. Detail dismissal returns focus to the front card when it remains mounted without losing collection position. Respect reduced motion and visible keyboard focus. Rendered screenshots and measured geometry, not class inspection alone, decide visual acceptance.

## Compatibility, Rollout and Reversal

This is a presentational client change over existing summary and schedule data. Existing search criteria, GET/POST paging, card identity, detail loading, planner assignment and collection position persist through the visual change. The plan has no migration, new dependency, backend or production deployment. Reversal restores the two card renderings without data repair.
