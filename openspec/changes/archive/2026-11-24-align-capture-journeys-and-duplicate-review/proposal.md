# Proposal

## Why

Current recipe capture, sharing import, async feedback, and recovery surfaces have accumulated cross-screen friction and visual drift: photo, URL, description, and `.txt` imports trap household members on intermediate waiting or confirmation screens instead of returning immediately to Home (`pwa/src/components/capture/MinimalCapture.tsx`); PDF confirmation hides the top-right cancel exit (`pwa/src/app/(app)/layout.tsx`); duplicate warnings jump the form layout or vanish after background extraction; shared `.txt` imports render a confusing `Original` badge, inline wall-of-steps, and read-only scrubbed metadata instead of letting the importer record their own rating and notes; Settings failed-capture clearing relies on a native browser `window.confirm()` popup (`pwa/src/components/profile/FailedCapturesSection.tsx`); and `LibraryToast`, `RecipeFailureBanner`, `DemoAiActionGate`, and `RecipeImportIssueSheet` retain Era 2 stripes, duplicate dismiss icons, bare 44px links, checkmarked solid-red pills, and English-only strings.

## Outcome and Scope

Deliver the unified Era 3 Family Cookbook Capture experience specified in `docs/visual-language/designs/capture-visual-design-suite.svg` and `docs/visual-language/designs/capture-journeys.svg` with the smallest possible code, contract, and persistence footprint:

1. **Save-and-Return-Home across all capture modes:** Photo, URL, description, PDF, and shared `.txt` imports return directly to `/home` once intake is accepted, retiring intermediate `/capture` countdown/done screens while surfacing a quiet ambient in-flight status ribbon on Home (`Preparing N recipe(s) in the background` with a Hearth pulsing dot and secondary `+ Add another` link).
2. **One coherent vertical grammar & universal `[×]` cancel on `/capture`:** Every capture screen uses an open-cream left-aligned heading and a top-right `48×48` white circular `[×]` cancel control (including PDF confirmation, with no header divider line and no separate `Back` button), clean Entry Hub camera CTA (removing the extra drop-hint text and legacy `.recipe` label), conditional Sunlight duplicate advisory directly below the source input (zero layout shift while typing), shared `RecipeDetailSheet` appreciation rating (`👎 Dislike / 👍 Like / ❤️ Love`) and notes, and a single primary Hearth CTA.
3. **Hero-led shared `.txt` review with inline `Preview steps` disclosure:** Shared `.txt` bundle review preserves the generous hero image, recipe name, filename, and original photo count, replaces the wall of instructions with an inline `Preview steps` / `Hide preview` disclosure using `RecipeDetailSheet` Garden section headings and numbered sage step badges, removes the `Original` / `Synthesized` pill, and provides editable importer `Rating` and `Notes`.
4. **Automatic workflow duplicate flagging & Paired Duplicate Review:** Finalizing a background or shared-bundle import checks ready non-deleted library recipes for normalized URL or title/ingredient overlap and, when matched without an existing report, records a standard `duplicate` `RecipeImportReport` without deleting either recipe or altering database tables or workflow YAML graphs. `RecipeImportIssueDto` exposes an optional live-resolved `suspectedDuplicateRecipeId` so household members can open a Paired Duplicate Review sheet directly from `LibraryToast`, `Recipes` review filters, or `RecipeDetailSheet` and choose `Keep both recipes`, `Keep existing · move new to Bin`, or `Keep new · move existing to Bin`.
5. **App-coherent upload, error, recovery, demo, and issue-review surfaces:** Align >800ms photo upload and PDF active saving copy to calm North Star language (`We’ll take you home as soon as they’re saved.` / `We’ll take you home as soon as your PDF is saved.`, with zero em-dashes), migrate `LibraryToast` and `RecipeFailureBanner` off `border-l-4` stripes and `+1 more` / duplicate `[×]` chrome, replace `FailedCapturesSection` `window.confirm()` with an inline `Keep` + danger-blush `Clear capture` confirmation and separated `Retry` / trailing `Trash` layout, upgrade `DemoAiActionGate` to a `48px` secondary-styled action, and migrate `RecipeImportIssueSheet` to Era 3 blush reason pills (without checkmarks) and full EN/FR localization.

**Exclusions:**
- No new database tables, columns, or `api/database/compatibility.sql` migrations (`recipe_import_reports` already supports `reasons = ['duplicate']` with nullable member/workflow references under `CurrentRecoveryHouseholdState`).
- No new YAML workflow task nodes in `recipe-import.yaml`, `url-import.yaml`, or `goto-synthesis.yaml` (preserving `WorkflowProcessorHistory` and backup/recovery journal invariants).
- No recipe "archive" state; duplicate cleanup reuses existing soft-delete (`Move to Bin` / `RecycleBinSheet`) and `DELETE /api/recipes/{id}/import-report`.

## What Changes

- **OpenAPI & DTO seam (`specs/openapi.yaml`, `api/src/RecipeApi/Dto/RecipeImportIssueDto.cs`):** Add optional nullable `suspectedDuplicateRecipeId` (`uuid | null`) to `RecipeImportIssueDto` (resolved live against ready non-deleted recipes when `reasons` includes `duplicate`), and include optional `isSuspectedDuplicate` boolean on `recipe_ready` SSE payloads when a finalized import has a `duplicate` report.
- **Backend duplicate flagging (`api/src/RecipeApi/Services/`):** Add a focused deterministic duplicate detector reusing `RecipeSearchDocumentBuilder` normalization and ingredient Jaccard scoring; invoke it during `RecipeReadyProcessor` finalization (before `PublishRecipeReadyAsync`, preserving `RecipeReadyProcessor`'s exact returned task receipt) and `RecipeService.ImportRecipeShareBundle` to create a `Reported` `RecipeImportReport` (`reasons = ["duplicate"]`) when a match is found and no report exists.
- **Unified `/capture` shell & shared rating (`pwa/src/app/(app)/layout.tsx`, `pwa/src/components/capture/`, `pwa/src/components/recipes/RecipeRatingControl.tsx`):** Extract the borderless-on-cream / raised-white `RecipeDetailSheet` rating selector for reuse across Detail, Photo, URL, PDF, and `.txt` capture; replace `/capture`'s `.glass-header` with an open-cream left-aligned header + universal `48×48` white circular `[×]` cancel across all modes (including PDF confirmation); remove intermediate post-save waiting/success screens so all accepted intakes navigate straight to `/home`.
- **Shared `.txt` review, North Star upload copy, and Demo gate (`pwa/src/components/capture/MinimalCapture.tsx`, `PdfCaptureConfirmation.tsx`, `pwa/src/components/demo/DemoAiActionGate.tsx`):** Update `.txt` bundle review to hero + filename + original count + `Preview steps` disclosure + below-card duplicate advisory + editable importer Rating & Notes; update >800ms photo overlay and PDF active saving copy; style `DemoAiActionGate`'s prepared-recipes link as a `48px` Era 3 secondary button.
- **Home async feedback, Settings inline confirm, Paired Duplicate Review & Era 3 Issue Sheet (`pwa/src/components/home/`, `LibraryToast.tsx`, `RecipeFailureBanner.tsx`, `FailedCapturesSection.tsx`, `RecipeImportIssueSheet.tsx`, `PairedDuplicateReviewSheet.tsx`, `RecipeDetailSheet.tsx`):** Render the calm Home in-flight capture ribbon when `captureStore.pendingRecipes` is non-empty; restyle `LibraryToast` (duplicate-aware prompt, no left stripe or `+1 more` pill) and `RecipeFailureBanner` (Board 02 Hearth recovery card, single `Dismiss`); replace `window.confirm()` in `FailedCapturesSection`; add Paired Duplicate Review (`Keep both recipes`, `Keep existing · move new to Bin`, `Keep new · move existing to Bin`); and migrate `RecipeImportIssueSheet` to Era 3 tokens, uncheckmarked blush pills, and EN/FR locale keys.

### Modified Capabilities

- `recipe-capture`: Unify `/capture` header and universal `[×]` cancel (including PDF confirmation), standardize shared `RecipeDetailSheet` appreciation rating, place duplicate advisories below source inputs, return straight to `/home` after accepted intake with an ambient Home in-flight ribbon, update active upload/error and `DemoAiActionGate` treatments, restyle `LibraryToast` and `RecipeFailureBanner`, and broadcast duplicate-flagged readiness.
- `recipe-sharing`: Scope user-facing shared file copy to `.txt`, present hero-led bundle review with an inline `Preview steps` Garden disclosure (without `Original`/`Synthesized` pills), collect the importer's own appreciation rating and notes on import, flag suspected duplicates on import, and return directly to `/home` upon acceptance.
- `capture-recovery`: Replace native `window.confirm()` in Settings `FailedCapturesSection` with an inline Era 3 confirmation (`Keep` left, danger-blush `Clear capture` right), separate `Retry` (`flex-1`) from the trailing `Trash` icon button, and align `RecipeFailureBanner` to the single-dismiss Board 02 Hearth recovery card.
- `recipe-quality`: Automatically create a `duplicate` `RecipeImportReport` when finalized imports match an existing ready recipe, expose live-resolved `suspectedDuplicateRecipeId` on `RecipeImportIssueDto`, provide Paired Duplicate Review (`Keep both recipes` or `Move to Bin` via Recycle Bin) from Toast, Recipes review filters, and Recipe Detail, and migrate `RecipeImportIssueSheet` to Era 3 styling (blush pills without checkmarks) and EN/FR localization.
