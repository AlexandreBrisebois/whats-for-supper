# Proposal

## Why

The shared quality form can initialize from cooking context while retaining saved feedback. The promoted baseline specifies ordinary detail initialization, while the connected cooking draft owns its entry and local return; this remaining quality concern needs a distinct owner.

## What Changes

- Add contextual reason merge and note retention, including a saved Duplicate mixed with context.
- Reuse promoted reason toggling and server manual-only policy; retain U75.
- Map all scenarios to BSL-recipe-quality-017 in `docs/openspec-baseline/recipe-quality.md`.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `recipe-quality`: Add shared issue-form contextual initialization only.

## Impact

Documentation only. Evidence spans RecipeImportIssueSheet.mergeReasons, CooksMode props, recipes.ts/generated POST, approved import-report contract, controller/report service and persistence/workflow policy. The concurrently generated `baseline-cooking-edit-report-handoffs` owns cooking entry, local return and acknowledgement; those requirements are not duplicated here. Existing promoted quality requirements own subsequent edits, validation, submission, attempt policy and manual resolution. A34 and U01/U75–U88 remain. No application changes, promotion or archive; no consequential unsettled choice blocks extraction.
