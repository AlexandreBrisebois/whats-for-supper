# Proposal

## Why

Tonight gives ordinary empty nights a generous illustration but recipe-free Take Out, Eating out and custom plans remain compact summaries. Mystery without a ready GOTO has no explanation of why no recipe is shown. A consistent plan card should make the saved intention recognizable at a glance and give one useful next action.

## Outcome and Scope

Use the ordinary empty pivot's large-icon composition for recipe-free saved plans and Mystery without a ready proposal. Preserve the completed, unarchived `show-mystery-night-recipe-hero` ready-Mystery photograph/header and explicit acceptance. Rename public Order In copy to Take Out throughout the app, keeping compatible internal identifiers and existing delivery support. The approved exception is explicit Mystery → Take Out replacement through the existing dated non-cooking save. No new API or scheduling policy.

The design uses generic unavailable-suggestion copy because the existing active-GOTO client collapses empty eligibility and transport failure. It must not assert that no recipes are starred. French proposed wording is « À emporter », with « À emporter ou en livraison » as the existing explanatory scope; this is a reversible content choice, not a removal of delivery.

## What Changes

- Replace the small Home saved-plan summary with the normal cookbook card shell, large corresponding icon, saved title/note and explicit Edit supper plan navigation.
- Give Mystery without a ready proposal a generous Sparkles composition and a short truthful explanation, retaining Quick Find and Take Out. Keep checking/preparing feedback honest and suppress false readiness.
- Keep ready recipes' existing 5:4 photo and modest failed-image fallback, including ready Mystery.
- Rename all application-owned visible Order In labels, accessible names and related action copy in both locales. Update current guidance and copy assertions; leave enums, payloads, test IDs, archived/historical evidence and user-entered content alone.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `tonight-meal-decisions`: coherent recipe-free Tonight presentation and truthful Mystery availability explanation; global public-copy rule for Take Out.
- `non-cooking-plans`: expanded Home saved-plan card preserving the existing Planner handoff and custom content.

## Impact

Affected owners: `HomeCommandCenter`, `TonightPivotCard`, shared Tonight shell, `NonCookingIcon`, localized non-cooking label helper, non-cooking/recovery/planning sheets, EN/FR dictionaries, relevant unit/browser assertions and current user/design guidance. No contract/client generation, backend, database migration, notification protocol, dependency or telemetry work. See [design](design.md) for the source-backed matrix and rename inventory. Existing OTel removal and Mystery hero edits are outside this change's ownership.
