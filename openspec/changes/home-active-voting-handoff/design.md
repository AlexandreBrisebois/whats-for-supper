## Decisions and Tradeoffs

1. Derive Home's card directly from the shared session, not Planner selection, scheduled days or a second local voting model. Request a coalesced session refresh on Home entry so initial hydration does not depend on successful SSE construction. Reuse the layout stream thereafter and the same refresh action for Retry. Add no poller or additional stream subscription.
2. Retain the existing Home card location, warmth and touch-sized primary action. Collecting uses neutral week-independent copy ("The family is voting") and Vote Now. Alex approved the exact English converged headline "Menu's In", supporting copy "Your matches are ready to review and save." and CTA "See your matches". Preserve equivalent French intent. Use Home-specific locale keys where reuse would change Discovery copy, capitalization, punctuation or description. Remove the misleading recipe-day count. Do not claim that the plan is saved, locked or voting closed.
3. Remove dismissal while a round is open. Round-scoped dismissal is a credible quieter alternative, but would still violate the user's request to show the invitation whenever voting is open. No dismissal state is needed. Reopened rounds follow their new authoritative identity rather than inheriting completion or suppression from an earlier round.
4. For status `unknown` or initial `loading` without usable accepted data, show compact "Checking voting…" with no round CTA. During an ordinary background recheck retain a usable last-confirmed card and enabled read-only navigation action, adding checking feedback. After failure, retain that usable action with "Voting status unavailable", explicit last-confirmed context and Retry; failure is not fresh confirmation. Without a valid retained identity/destination, withhold round navigation and offer Retry after failure. Accepted unowned state removes the invitation; an accepted replacement round updates it without preserving the prior action. No poller, TTL, extra stream or parallel freshness state machine is proposed.
5. At activation, read the latest accepted usable open-round snapshot, including retained data during `loading` or `unavailable`; capture its round and absolute Monday once. Recheck usability so an accepted close/replacement cannot leave an old action active. For converged, set Planner tab and navigate to `/planner?weekOffset=0&referenceWeekStartDate=<captured Monday>`. Do not recompute a relative offset from the browser date. Discovery or Planner reconciles destination state; a server close racing after activation is not hidden by this handoff. Navigation grants no mutation rights and promises no atomic read across clients.

Alex approved the supporting copy and usable background navigation policy in the follow-up review. Those decisions and the narrow shared-store failure outcome below are explicit reviewed scope. The later "build it" authorization permits their implementation; it does not permit unrelated changes. If the design changes, reconcile the delta and tasks first.

## State, Concurrency and Recovery

Reuse accepted session revisions and incarnations; Home must not compare wall clocks, derive convergence from local vote counts, copy suggestion arrays or relax existing stale-frame guards. Read failures retain shared last-known data but never establish newly confirmed closed or converged state. Retry, reconnect and mobile resume converge through the existing store. A new round must not inherit the prior round's converged action. Home routes only from a retained accepted round with a usable absolute Monday; it never routes from an incoming rejected projection or guesses a destination.

Technical review note, verified against current `discoveryStore.applySession`: malformed identity/week projections return silently. An invalid authoritative HTTP result can leave `sessionStatus` loading; an invalid SSE frame can leave prior ready data intact. Home cannot infer unavailable/Retry for every rejected frame. Retain validation with the existing store owner. The smallest necessary shared-store change is to give the completing current authoritative read a rejected/unavailable outcome when that owner cannot accept its projection, while retaining last accepted data. Use existing generation/revision/incarnation guards so a superseded read or stale rejection cannot overwrite a newer accepted status. Do not reinterpret a valid older/duplicate snapshot as a malformed failure; existing idempotent acceptance stays intact. Resolve the read once; no automatic retry loop is added. Scope this outcome and its focused store tests explicitly in tasks.

Malformed SSE rejection continues to leave accepted data/status intact and is not promised to show Retry immediately. Recovery remains the existing authoritative reconciliation on entry, reconnect, resume or explicit Retry after a reported failure. New-incarnation frames still require HTTP confirmation; they do not replace the last accepted baseline. A failed confirmation can leave last-confirmed navigation usable with unavailable feedback. No generic validator, transport changes or second Home session model is included.

Test invariants: every usable accepted collecting/converged snapshot maps to the appropriate action independently of week selection, including during ordinary rechecking and failed refreshes; accepted unowned maps to none; missing usable accepted identity/destination maps to no action. A malformed current HTTP read settles unavailable; superseded rejection cannot overwrite newer accepted status; ignored SSE alone does not manufacture unavailable state. Navigation retains the captured Monday across date rollover; handoff triggers no assignment, lock or close request. Use existing test tools; no new property-testing framework is needed.

## User Experience and Design Review

Benefit: a household can resume the vote from Home and leave quickly for useful matches once enough agreement exists. One familiar card and one phase-appropriate action reduce interpretation and preserve the existing quick convergence win. Reuse Solar Earth tokens, semantic controls, focus visibility and reduced-motion behavior; keep Tonight usable during status work. Verify wrapping, reachable controls and English/French meaning on iPad, iPhone and Pixel Phone layouts. Exact approved English Home headline has no exclamation mark; do not silently inherit Discovery's different styling/copy.

Trade-offs: the persistent invitation occupies Home space, and usable last-confirmed navigation can reach a round that changed while disconnected. Checking/unavailable feedback and destination reconciliation make that limitation clear while preserving a smooth read-only handoff. Withhold only when no usable accepted destination remains or newer accepted state supersedes it. Avoid an extra modal or full-screen celebration on Home.

Resolved copy decision: "Menu's In" can imply a finished, saved menu, so Alex approved "Your matches are ready to review and save." beneath it. Saving and voting closure remain explicit existing Planner actions. No automatic closure is implied or authorized. The later application authorization includes the bounded shared-store invalid-HTTP completion outcome and focused tests described above; no consequential product choice remains unresolved.

Baseline caution: the local older recipe-discovery spec still describes a celebration without a CTA. Current main's recipe-discovery spec has already been updated to the green success card and See your matches action, agreeing with current main's Discovery source. Use that current-main evidence; do not manufacture a discrepancy or alter Discovery in this Home change. Home's approved exact headline and supporting sentence are intentionally local to Home.
