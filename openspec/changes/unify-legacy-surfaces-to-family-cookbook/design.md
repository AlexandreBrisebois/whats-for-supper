## Decisions and Tradeoffs

### 1. Entry, Shell & Brand Alignment (Wave A)
- **Welcome / Landing / Onboarding:** Replace `text-indigo`/`bg-indigo`/`text-lavender`/`bg-ochre text-white` with Family Cookbook tokens (`font-heading text-charcoal`, `<Button variant="primary" size="lg">`, `text-terracotta-700` alerts, `bg-cream`).
- **Navigation (`Navigation.tsx`):** Move `NAV_ITEMS` localization inside the render path using `useLocale()` so locale toggles update tab labels cleanly without reload; raise tab label size to `text-xs` (`12px`), inactive color to `text-charcoal-600`, and Discover orb text to `text-charcoal` on `bg-ochre` + `text-ochre-900` label below. *(Review Checkpoint 1: confirm `h-16 w-16` vs `h-20 w-20` Discover orb dimensions.)*
- **Header (`Header.tsx`):** Drop `"Command Center"` and `"Matchmaking"` micro-subtitles and localize greeting text through `locales/{en,fr}/common.json`.

### 2. Making *"Swipe, Match, Cook!"* Richer & Clearer for Families (Wave B)
- **`DiscoveryCard.tsx` (`line-clamp-2` description):** Render `description?.trim()` in `text-sm font-medium text-charcoal-600 line-clamp-2` beneath the `font-heading text-2xl font-bold text-charcoal` recipe title, omitting the element completely when `description` is empty/whitespace. Reduce `h-[62%]` hero image proportionally (`h-[58%]`) on tall cards only when description is present, preserving image dominance and zero cuisine/meal-type pill clutter (`recipe-discovery` spec constraint).
- **Voting Overlays & Controls:** Replace `bg-sage/30` (`LOVE` overlay) with `bg-sage-700/90 text-white` for crisp legibility over bright photography; change `Just planned ✓` badge to `bg-sage-50 border-sage-200 text-sage-800`; switch Like/Dislike thumb-zone buttons from bare `text-sage` / `text-terracotta` to `text-sage-700` / `text-terracotta-700`; change empty-state primary CTA and `FamilyNudgeDialog` Share button from `bg-ochre text-white` to `bg-terracotta text-cream-100` (or `bg-ochre text-charcoal`).

### 3. Retiring `QuickFindModal` 3D Flip & Unifying Planner/Capture Sheets (Wave C)
- **Inline Disclosure in `QuickFindModal.tsx`:** Replace the `perspective-1000` / `rotate-y-180` / `backface-hidden` two-sided card flip with a scrollable Family Cookbook card (matching `TonightMenuCard` and `DiscoveryCard` proportions) featuring an explicit `min-h-12` button (`View details` / `Hide details` with `aria-expanded` and `ChevronDown`) that expands `description` and `ingredients` inline while keeping the recipe photo, title, and `Select` / `Use for this night` button visible and immediately actionable.
- **Planner Participation Buttons & Micro-Text:** Upgrade `Ask the Family` and `Nudge family` in `planner/page.tsx` from `bg-sage text-[9px] text-white` to `bg-sage-700 text-xs font-semibold text-white` (7.22:1, Review Checkpoint 4) and upgrade remaining `9px`–`11px` labels across `planner`, `PlanningPivotSheet`, `SkipRecoveryDialog`, and `MinimalCapture` to `>= 12px` sentence case.

### 4. Settings, Recycle Bin & Ratchet Closure (Wave D)
- Replace `red-50/100/200/500/600/700` in `RecycleBinSheet.tsx` with `terracotta-50`/`terracotta-200`/`terracotta-700` destructive pairings (`>= 48px` Restore, Delete, Cancel, and Close buttons).
- Raise `FailedCapturesSection` clear button to `h-12 w-12` (`48px`) and Retry to `min-h-12` `text-xs`.
- Remove every entry from `LEGACY_ERA_ALLOWLIST` in `pwa/src/test/visual-contract.test.ts` so `visual-contract.test.ts` enforces 100% app-wide compliance, then recapture `docs/screenshots/demo-*.png` from the live Demo state.

## First Observable Slice

Wave A (`WelcomeForm.tsx`, `onboarding/page.tsx`, `page.tsx`, `Header.tsx`, `Navigation.tsx`) removes the highest-visibility Entry/Shell Era 1 tokens (`indigo`, `pink`, `bg-ochre text-white`, `Command Center`, `9px`/`11px` nav), clears those 6+ files from `LEGACY_ERA_ALLOWLIST`, and verifies cleanly through `visual-contract.test.ts`, `Navigation.test.tsx`, `welcome/page.test.tsx`, and `visual-a11y-contract`.

## Compatibility, Rollout and Reversal

- **Test IDs and `aria-label` contracts preserved:** Retain all `data-testid`s (`discovery-card`, `like-button`, `dislike-button`, `quick-find-modal`, `recovery-dialog-title`, `action-restore-*`, `action-purge-*`, etc.) and semantic accessibility labels; update only unit/E2E assertions explicitly expecting removed 3D-flip behavior, missing `description`, or legacy red/ochre classes.
- **Locale completeness:** Any newly extracted strings (`Header`, `HomeGreeting`, `MinimalCapture`, `QuickFindModal`) add matching EN and FR keys verified by `locale-integrity.test.ts`.

## User Experience

- **For kids & family during Voting:** Seeing a brief 1–2 line appetizing description beneath each dish photo makes *"Swipe, Match, Cook!"* meaningful even when recipes have shorthand family names.
- **For tired cooks in Quick Find & Recovery:** Replacing the disorienting 3D card flip and `"RECOVERY FLOW"` micro-chrome with inline cookbook disclosure and calm sentence-case choices lowers cognitive strain at 5:00 PM.
- **For the whole app:** Emptying `LEGACY_ERA_ALLOWLIST` ensures no forgotten screen (`InviteLinkDialog`, `RecycleBinSheet`, `SubmitConfirmation`, `error.tsx`) breaks the warm Family Cookbook feel.
